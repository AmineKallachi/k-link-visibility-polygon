k-link visibility with a mirror

An interactive explanation of Mahdavi, Mohades & Kouhestani, Computing k-Link Visibility Polygons in Environments with a Reflective Edge (CCCG 2011).

All three canvases below share the same room and sensor: draw or change the room in any of them.

1. What does the sensor see?

Drag the sensor: the coloured region is everything it sees directly, without a mirror.

2. Adding a mirror

One wall is a mirror: the sensor also sees what its mirror image q′ sees through that wall.

3. Looking around corners: k links

With k links, a line of sight may bend k − 1 times; switch the mirror on for the goal of this project.
